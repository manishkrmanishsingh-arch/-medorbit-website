## 📌 About MedOrbit

**MedOrbit** is a digital education and admission guidance platform designed to help students make informed decisions about medical education in India and abroad.

The platform brings together counselling support, destination comparison, university information, documentation guidance, fee planning, and student assistance in one accessible and responsive website.

MedOrbit focuses on:

- Transparent admission guidance
- Student-first counselling
- Clear comparison of colleges and destinations
- Structured documentation assistance
- Mobile-friendly access
- Reliable post-enquiry support

---

## 🎯 Vision

To make quality medical education more accessible through transparent counselling, verified information, and technology-driven student support.

## 💡 Mission

- Simplify the admission journey
- Help students compare universities and destinations
- Provide clear information about eligibility and fees
- Support students with documentation and applications
- Deliver professional assistance before and after admission
- Build a trusted digital platform for future medical professionals
