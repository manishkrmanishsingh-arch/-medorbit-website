## 🌍 About

MedOrbit is a modern educational consultancy platform designed to guide students through the complete admission journey for MBBS in India and abroad. The platform focuses on transparency, accurate information, and a seamless digital experience.

### 🎯 Vision
To make quality medical education accessible through trusted counselling and technology.

### 💡 Mission
- Simplify admissions
- Compare universities
- Provide transparent guidance
- Deliver professional student support
