Document Checklist

Keep These Documents Ready

Exact requirements depend on the course and institution.

📚 Academic Documents

  • Class 10 marksheet and certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet and certificate
  • Graduation documents, if applicable
  • Entrance examination scorecard
  • Transfer or migration certificate

🪪 Identity Documents

  • Aadhaar card or accepted ID
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Birth certificate, where required
  • Passport for overseas admission
  • Parent or guardian ID documents

📑 Category & Other Documents

  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • Income or EWS certificate
  • Domicile or residence certificate
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Gap affidavit, where required

🌍 Overseas Documents

  • Valid passport
  • University offer or admission letter
  • Visa application documents
  • Financial proof
  • Attestation or apostille, if required

✅ Before Submission

  • Check spelling of name and date of birth
  • Keep clear scanned copies
  • Use the required file format and size
  • Keep original documents safely
  • Save application and payment receipts

⚠️ Avoid These Errors

  • Uploading unclear documents
  • Missing deadlines
  • Using incorrect category details
  • Paying through unofficial channels
  • Ignoring official instructions

Important Advice

Common Admission Mistakes to Avoid

⏰

Missing Deadlines

Save important dates and check official portals regularly.

💳

Unofficial Payments

Pay only through the authorised institution or counselling portal.

🏫

Unverified Institutions

Check recognition and course approval before applying.

📄

Document Errors

Check names, dates, categories and certificate validity.

📋

Poor Choice Filling

Arrange college preferences carefully before locking them.

💰

Ignoring Total Cost

Consider tuition, hostel, travel and additional expenses.

🗣️

Depending on Rumours

Use official notices instead of unverified social media messages.

🔐

Sharing Login Details

Protect passwords, OTPs and application credentials.
